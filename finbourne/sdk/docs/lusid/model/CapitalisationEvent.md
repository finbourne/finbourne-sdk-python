# CapitalisationEvent

Capitalisation (CAPI, AMI-SeCo SCoRE section 4.10): an increase in the current principal of a debt instrument  without an increase in its nominal value, normally from incorporating due but unpaid interest into the  principal. A notified CAPI on a ComplexBond, recording the factor before (previousFactor) and after  (nextFactor) the event. Mandatory and purely informational: it generates no cash flow, movements or  transactions. The capitalisation itself is booked by the bond's PikSchedule.
## Properties

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|------|------|----------|-------------|
| **effective_date** | **datetime** | Optional | The date the next factor applies (:98A::EFFD). Required. |
| **payment_date** | **datetime** | Optional | The payment date (:98A::PAYD). Optional and informational; it may fall before effectiveDate. |
| **record_date** | **datetime** | Optional | The record date (:98A::RDTE). Optional and informational; when supplied it must not fall after  effectiveDate. |
| **previous_factor** | **float** | Optional | The factor, current principal per unit of nominal, in force before the event (:92A::PRFC). Optional, but  supplied together with nextFactor or not at all; greater than 0 when supplied. An event without the  factors is stored as economically incomplete. |
| **next_factor** | **float** | Optional | The factor, current principal per unit of nominal, in force from effectiveDate (:92A::NWFC). Optional, but  supplied together with previousFactor or not at all; not less than previousFactor when supplied. |
| **interest_rate** | **float** | Optional | The annual interest rate capitalised, as a fraction, so 6 per cent is 0.06 (:92A::INTP). Optional and  informational; not negative when supplied. |
| **currency** | **str** | Optional | The currency of the capitalised principal (:11A::DENO). Optional and informational. |
