# BondForwardCashSettlementEvent

Cash settlement of a BondForward at its settlement date:  the forward closes against a single net payment of the clean price difference, and no bond changes  hands. Unlike a commodity forward the underlying is a mastered, quoted instrument, so LUSID computes  the settlement amount from the underlying's quote rather than taking a pre-netted figure; a supplied  referencePrice wins and the quote is not consulted.  Accrued does not enter the payoff, by cancellation rather than entitlement: both sides of the  difference accrue to the same date, so the accrued is identical on each and drops out.
## Properties

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|------|------|----------|-------------|
| **agreed_clean_price** | **float** | Required | The agreed price, percent of par, carried from the definition. |
| **contract_size** | **float** | Required | Face amount per unit, carried from the definition. It scales the payoff, so it is rejected where it  differs from the instrument&#39;s own value. |
| **maturity_date** | **datetime** | Optional | The forward&#39;s contractual settlement date, on which the price difference fixes. |
| **reference_price** | **float** | Optional | The underlying&#39;s clean price, percent of par, at the settlement date. Null resolves from the  underlying&#39;s quote; a supplied value wins and the quote is not consulted. |
| **settlement_amount_per_unit** | **float** | Optional | Output only: the net settlement per unit, computed from the reference price, the agreed price and  the contract size. A supplied value is overwritten. Negative is valid and means the holder pays. |
| **settlement_currency** | **str** | Required | Currency the net amount settles in, being the forward&#39;s domestic currency. |
| **settlement_date** | **datetime** | Optional | The date the net payment settles. Null means the maturity date; cash-settled forwards commonly pay  a day or two after the price fixes. Rejected where earlier than the maturity date. |
