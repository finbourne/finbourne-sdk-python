# PartialRedemptionWithoutReductionOfNominalValueEvent

Partial Redemption Without Reduction of Nominal Value (PRED, AMI-SeCo SCoRE section 4.47): securities are  redeemed in part before their scheduled final maturity date, the redemption is reflected in a pool factor  reduction and no movement of securities occurs. A notified PRED on an asset-backed ComplexBond. The holder  keeps its original face (units), the pool factor falls from previousFactor to nextFactor and the current face  falls with it; principalPerUnit of original face is repaid at pricePerUnit, par when omitted. Mandatory  participation only.
## Properties

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|------|------|----------|-------------|
| **ex_date** | **datetime** | Optional | The entitlement date of the paydown. A PRED notification carries a record date (:98A::RDTE) and no ex-date;  the record date goes here. Required. |
| **payment_date** | **datetime** | Optional | The date the principal is paid and the date the next factor takes effect (:98A::PAYD). Required; on or  after exDate. |
| **currency** | **str** | Required | The ISO 4217 code of the currency in which the principal is paid (:11A::OPTN). Required. |
| **principal_per_unit** | **float** | Optional | The principal repaid per unit of original face (AMI-SeCo RATE, per 1 of original face; a percentage is  divided by 100). Required and strictly positive. It may differ from previousFactor minus nextFactor,  because a write-off or an interest deferral in the same period moves the factor too. |
| **previous_factor** | **float** | Optional | The pool factor in force until paymentDate (:92A::PRFC). Required, together with nextFactor, so the  notified pool factor reduction can be reconciled against the custodian&#39;s factor file. |
| **next_factor** | **float** | Optional | The pool factor in force from paymentDate (:92A::NWFC). Required, together with previousFactor; strictly  positive and below previousFactor. |
| **price_per_unit** | **float** | Optional | The price per 1 of principal repaid (:90A::OFFR or :90B::OFFR), with 1 representing par; PRCT/98 is sent as  0.98. Optional; a null is read as 1, so an omitted price is par. |
